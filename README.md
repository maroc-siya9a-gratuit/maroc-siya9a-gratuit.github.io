### كيفاش تخدم بيه؟
1. **من التيليفون ولا الحاسوب:** دخل لـ [الموقع](https://maroc-siya9a-gratuit.github.io)، اختار الصنف ديالك ومن بعد السلسلة.
2. **دوز السلسلة بحال الامتحان:** سؤال بسؤال، كتسمع السؤال وكتشوف الصورة، كتختار الجواب (1 حتى 4، ولا جوج إلا كانو صحاح) وكتورك على «تأكيد»: كيبان ليك واش صبتي مع الشرح. فاللخر كتشوف النتيجة (خاصك 32 من 40). التقدّم ديالك كيتسجّل فالجهاز ديالك، وتقدر تكمّل من فين وقفتي.
3. **الإشارات:** كل إشارة بالمعنى والصوت، وفيها وضع «اختبر راسك» كيخبّي المعاني.
4. **قلّب على أي حاجة** من الخانة ديال البحث فالصفحة الرئيسية: إشارة، كلمة من الشرح، ولا موضوع (مثلاً: الأسبقية، الوقوف، حزام السلامة).
5. **بلا أنترنت:** ورك فوق على **Code ← Download ZIP**، حلّ الملف، وحلّ `index.html` فالمتصفح. كلشي كيخدم أوفلاين.

## 🇬🇧 English

### What is this?
A free, no-signup website to prepare for the **Moroccan driving licence theory exam** (*Code de la route Maroc*). Every question comes with its picture and a spoken version in Moroccan Darija; the correct answer stays hidden until you have tried, then you reveal it together with an explanation.

**Live site: [maroc-siya9a-gratuit.github.io](https://maroc-siya9a-gratuit.github.io)**

### What's inside

| Section | Count | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Exam series | 154 series · 5,379 questions | Car (B), Motorcycle (A), Truck (C), Bus (D), Truck with trailer (EC), Mechanics |
| Road signs | 249 signs | Danger, priority, prohibition, obligation, information, temporary, direction, additional — each with picture, meaning and audio |
| Lessons | 32 lessons | Traffic rules, road markings, accidents & first aid, and more |
| Tips | 478 tips | Grouped by topic, each with a picture and an explanation |
| Road cases | 72 cases | Real traffic situations, explained |
| Q&A | 23 | About the licence and the exam |

### How to use it
1. **Online:** open the [site](https://maroc-siya9a-gratuit.github.io) on a phone or a computer and pick a series.
2. **Take a series like the real exam:** one question at a time, with its picture and audio. Pick 1–4 (several if more than one is right), tap «تأكيد» (*confirm*) and see at once whether you were right, with the explanation. At the end you get your score (the pass mark is 32/40). Progress is kept on your own device, so you can resume where you stopped. A review view lists every question with its answer.
3. **Road signs** have a "test yourself" mode that hides the meanings until you tap a sign.
4. **Search** from the home page: a sign, a word from any explanation, or a topic. Arabic spelling variants are matched (e.g. `اشارة` finds `إشارة`).
5. **Offline:** **Code → Download ZIP**, unzip, open `index.html` in any browser. Every link is relative, so it works without internet.

### How it is built
Plain static HTML and CSS, one small script, no framework, no tracking, no cookies, no ads. Exam progress lives in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

```
index.html        home: search, licence types, signs, lessons, tips
pages/            a series picker per licence type; one exam page per series, sign group, lesson, tip group; cases; Q&A
media/            images (.webp/.gif) and audio (.m4a), loaded lazily, one page at a time
site.js           the exam player, progress, search box, audio buttons
search.js         search index, downloaded only when you first tap the search box
fonts/ style.css  IBM Plex Sans Arabic + Noto Kufi Arabic (OFL) and the single stylesheet (light + dark)
sitemap.xml       for search engines
```

Pages load only their own media, so a phone on mobile data never downloads more than the page it shows.
